BUSINESS OF THE LLP

Seven modules. One array.

Our scope is defined the way a solar array is built — individual modules, wired together, delivering one output. Each line of business below is a working part of every project we deliver.

Solar Panel Installation

Rooftop, ground-mount and structure-mounted PV installation for residential, commercial and industrial sites.

Solar Energy Systems & Infrastructure

Complete system design — arrays, inverters, mounting structures, metering and balance-of-system infrastructure.

EPC & Turnkey Solar Projects

Single point of accountability from engineering and procurement through construction and commissioning.

Solar Equipment Trading & Supply

Sourcing and supply of panels, inverters, batteries and mounting hardware from vetted manufacturers.

Electrical & Energy-Related Services

Wiring, switchgear, net-metering coordination and grid-interconnection work carried out to code.

Maintenance & Repair Services

Scheduled cleaning, performance monitoring, fault diagnosis and repair to protect system output over its lifetime.

Allied & Incidental Activities

02

03
